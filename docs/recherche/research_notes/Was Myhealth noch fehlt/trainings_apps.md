# Feature inventory of leading strength-training and workout apps (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: page fetching (WebFetch) was blocked in this session for several hosts (sensai.fit, alphaprogression.com could not be resolved), so all findings come from web search result summaries. Many sources are comparison blogs written by competing apps (Alpha Progression, Boostcamp, JEFIT, Hevy, SensAI, Push/Pull, Gymnoteplus and others) and are biased. Where possible, official pages (hevyapp.com, help.hevyapp.com, App Store listings, strava.com, support.runna.com, apple.com) are cited. Prices are US$ unless marked. Verified euro prices for Germany were mostly not found (see Gaps).

## 1. Logging (sets/reps/weight, RPE/RIR, set types, timers, calculators, watch logging)

### Takeaway
Baseline logging is a commodity. Every serious tracker (Hevy, Strong, Boostcamp, JEFIT, Alpha Progression, Liftosaur) logs sets, reps and weight with rest timers, and most offer RPE/RIR, supersets and set types (warm-up, drop, failure) for free. The paywall usually sits on calculators (Strong PRO and Liftosaur premium have plate calculators; Hevy's warm-up calculator is Pro) and on history depth, not on logging itself.

### Cited Findings
**Hevy**
- Set types: regular, drop, warm-up and failure sets. The type can be changed during a workout by tapping the set number (for example "D-Dropset"). The rest timer does not start if the next set is a drop set. For failure sets, you log the last completed rep. — [Hevy Help: Set Types](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/34896293707927-Set-Types-in-Hevy-Explained-Drop-Sets-Warm-Up-Sets-and-More); [Hevy: Workout set types](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/workout-set-types/)
- Supersets can hold more than two exercises. "Smart superset scrolling" jumps to the next exercise after a set is completed. — [Hevy: Supersets](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/what-are-supersets/)
- RPE is logged per set and turned on in settings. It cannot be set as a target when building a routine. — [Hevy: Workout settings](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/workout-settings/) (via search summary)
- The plate calculator is turned on in workout settings and opened from a calculator button above the keyboard. It does not appear to be Pro-gated. — [Hevy: Track workouts](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/track-workouts/); [Hevy: Workout settings](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/workout-settings/)
- The warm-up calculator is a Hevy Pro feature. — [Hevy Help 2026 Features Guide](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/33106320824727-Everything-You-Need-to-Know-About-the-Hevy-App-2026-Features-Guide); [SensAI Hevy review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/hevy-review-2026)
- Automatic rest timers are listed on the homepage. — [Hevy homepage](https://www.hevyapp.com/)
- On Apple Watch you can log sets, start rest timers and track heart rate, with live sync to the phone, on the free plan. Hevy is the app most consistently listed with Wear OS support as well. — [Hevy features](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/); [Hevy App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hevy-workout-tracker-gym-log/id1458862350); [Strive / FindYourEdge roundup](https://www.findyouredge.app/news/best-strength-training-apps-2026)
- Watch gaps reported by users: you cannot browse the routine while the rest timer runs on the watch. A rest-time setting bug (the value reverts after a change) and a drag-to-reorder bug that can drop the moved exercise were reported in App Store reviews. — [Hevy App Store reviews (watch)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hevy-workout-tracker-gym-log/id1458862350?see-all=reviews&platform=watch); [Push/Pull vs Hevy](https://push-pull.app/blog/push-pull-vs-hevy)

**Strong**
- The Apple Watch app logs with or without the iPhone, records heart rate and calories, and syncs live. One reviewer notes it covers only weights and reps on the wrist (no programs or progressions). Version 6.3.3 (30 Apr 2026) added an Apple Watch "Button Repeat" toggle. — [Strong App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strong-workout-tracker-gym-log/id464254577); [SensAI Strong review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/strong-app-review-2026); [Gymnoteplus Apple Watch apps](https://www.gymnoteplus.com/blog/best-workout-apps-for-apple-watch)
- PRO unlocks the plate calculator, warm-up calculator and advanced charts. — [SensAI Strong review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/strong-app-review-2026); [Push/Pull vs Strong](https://push-pull.app/blog/push-pull-vs-strong)
- Strong is generally listed as Apple Watch only (no Wear OS). — [FindYourEdge](https://www.findyouredge.app/news/best-strength-training-apps-2026)

**Alpha Progression** (German company)
- RIR tracking and RIR targets are both optional. Weight and rep recommendations work without RIR. — [Alpha Progression guide](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/alpha-progression-guide); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gym-workout-alpha-progression/id1462277793)
- An iPhone Live Activity shows the current exercise, set, recommendation and rest timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Android uses live notifications. There is no dedicated Apple Watch or Wear OS workout app. — [Alpha Progression homepage](https://alphaprogression.com/en); [Alpha Progression vs RP](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/alpha-progression-vs-rp-hypertrophy)

**Fitbod**
- On Apple Watch you can log sets, adjust reps and weight, and track rest and heart rate, with sync to the iPhone afterwards. No source confirms standalone use without an iPhone. — [Trainwell Apple Watch apps](https://www.trainwell.net/blog/personal-trainer-apps-apple-watch-progress-tracking); [Gymnoteplus](https://www.gymnoteplus.com/blog/best-workout-apps-for-apple-watch)
- Wear OS support is contested across sources. — [FindYourEdge](https://www.findyouredge.app/news/best-strength-training-apps-2026); [Alpha Progression best gym apps](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/best-gym-apps)

**Boostcamp**
- RPE and RIR logging, plate calculator, rest timers, PRs and estimated 1RMs are all free. — [Boostcamp free workout app](https://www.boostcamp.app/free-workout-app); [Boostcamp best free](https://www.boostcamp.app/best/free)

**Liftosaur**
- Premium covers graphs, week insights, the plate calculator and push notifications, and "the rest is free" according to Liftosaur's docs. The App Store and a review site instead list Liftoscript scripting, body measurements and periodization as premium (conflict). — [Liftosaur overview](https://www.liftosaur.com/blog/posts/liftosaur-overview/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/liftosaur-scriptable-workouts/id1661880849); [SaaSpartout](https://saaspartout.com/marketplace/liftosaur/)

**JEFIT**
- Watch app support (Apple Watch, and per several reviews Wear OS) is part of Elite. — [JustGains JEFIT alternatives](https://justgains.com/learn/jefit-alternatives); [FindYourEdge](https://www.findyouredge.app/news/best-strength-training-apps-2026)

**Ladder**
- Built-in timer, in-ear audio coaching, and Spotify / Apple Music integration in the workout. — [Ladder App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1502936453); [Gymadapt Ladder cost](https://gymadapt.com/learn/how-much-does-ladder-app-cost/)

**Strava**
- On 15 Oct 2026 Strava announced coach-designed strength workouts with live set and rep tracking. Older comparisons list no strength feature. — [the5krunner Runna vs Strava](https://the5krunner.com/2026/10/28/runna-vs-strava/)

### Inferences
- A German competitor needs, at minimum and for free: set types (warm-up, drop, failure), supersets with auto-advance, per-set RPE/RIR, auto rest timer, plate calculator, estimated 1RM and PRs. These are free in Hevy and Boostcamp, so charging for them is not competitive.
- Lock Screen Live Activity and Dynamic Island with the rest timer (as in Alpha Progression) and a phone-independent watch app (as in Strong) are differentiators.
- Unilateral (left/right) logging, assisted bodyweight, cardio-machine and timed-exercise logging were not specifically confirmed per app in the search results. Hevy's "exercise programming options" page covers exercise types, but its content was not retrievable.

### Gaps
- No confirmed per-app data on unilateral logging, assisted or weighted bodyweight types, distance/duration exercise types, or per-set notes (Hevy's exercise-programming-options page exists but could not be fetched: https://www.hevyapp.com/features/exercise-programming-options/).
- No source confirms standalone Wear OS logging for any app.
- Hevy Live Activity and widgets: mentioned only in a third-party summary, unconfirmed.

## 2. Programming (AI plans, auto-progression, periodization, recovery-adaptive volume, program libraries, substitutions, gym profiles)

### Takeaway
The market splits into three groups. (a) Algorithmic or AI coaches: Fitbod, Alpha Progression Pro, Hevy Trainer (Pro, launched Feb 2026), JEFIT Elite AI, Freeletics Coach. (b) Feedback-driven hypertrophy periodization: RP Hypertrophy, which sets volume from pump, soreness and workload feedback, plus Alpha Progression's deloads and periodization. (c) Program libraries and scripted programs: Boostcamp (14,000+ programs, mostly free), Liftosaur (Liftoscript, 50+ built-in), Ladder (coach team programs), Gymshark (450+ free workouts). Human coaching (Future, Caliber) is a separate high-price tier.

### Cited Findings
**Hevy**
- Hevy Trainer (Pro) gives personalized programs, progressive-overload suggestions, and suggested weights and reps. — [Hevy Help 2026 Features Guide](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/33106320824727-Everything-You-Need-to-Know-About-the-Hevy-App-2026-Features-Guide)
- Trainer onboarding asks for experience, goal, equipment, frequency, duration and muscle focus. It uses no sleep, HRV or readiness data. — [SensAI Hevy review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/hevy-review-2026); [AIToolsBakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/hevy-review/)
- Reported 2026 timeline (unofficial): Trainer launched Feb 2026. Later additions included HYROX exercises, gym tagging, and sending workouts to ChatGPT or Claude (around Sep 2026). — [SensAI Hevy review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/hevy-review-2026); [PRPath Hevy review](https://prpath.app/blog/hevy-app-review-2026.html)
- Before Trainer, critics said Hevy "won't help you build" a program. — [Dr-Muscle Hevy review](https://dr-muscle.com/hevy-workout-app-review/)

**Fitbod**
- Generates workouts algorithmically. Each muscle group gets a recovery score from 0 to 100%, shown as a heat map, which the user can override. Apple Health or Health Connect activities (runs, hikes) count toward fatigue. There are no programmed deload weeks and no HRV, sleep or readiness data. — [SensAI Fitbod review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/fitbod-review-2026); [AIToolsBakery Fitbod](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/fitbod-review/)
- Multiple equipment profiles for different gyms, home or travel, now called "Locations". — [Stronger vs Fitbod](https://www.strongermobileapp.com/compare/fitbod); [Fitnessdrum Fitbod review](https://fitnessdrum.com/fitbod-review/)

**Alpha Progression**
- Pro: an automatic plan generator using goal, experience, frequency, duration, equipment, available weights and muscle priorities. Progression recommendations per working set (weight, reps, optional RIR target), plus periodization and deloads. — [Alpha Progression Pro worth it](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/alpha-progression-pro-worth-it); [Alpha Progression guide](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/alpha-progression-guide)
- Free: unlimited logging and manual plan creation. — [Alpha Progression best muscle-building apps](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/best-muscle-building-apps)

**RP Hypertrophy**
- The Meso Builder adjusts weekly volume and load from feedback on pump, soreness, workload, joint pain and performance. It raises, holds or lowers set counts and uses its own scale instead of RPE or RIR. There are 45+ premade plans or custom mesocycles, and 250+ technique videos. — [Mesostrength RP review](https://mesostrength.com/blog/rp-hypertrophy-app-review); [Boostcamp vs RP](https://www.boostcamp.app/vs/rp-hypertrophy); [RP App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rp-hypertrophy/id1555614554)

**Boostcamp**
- 14,000+ programs: 130+ by coaches, the rest by users. Most coach programs are free. The custom program builder (in the app or on desktop) is free and unlimited. Pro adds "personalized programs", 20+ exclusive coach programs, and (per one review) auto progression. Some coach programs are sold as one-time purchases. — [Boostcamp program library](https://www.boostcamp.app/features/program-library); [Boostcamp Pro](https://www.boostcamp.app/pro); [Fortune Boostcamp review](https://fortune.com/article/boostcamp-review/)

**Liftosaur**
- Liftoscript is a plain-text program language that applies progression after each session. 50+ built-in programs (GZCLP, 5/3/1, PPL). — [Liftosaur](https://www.liftosaur.com/); [Liftosaur overview](https://www.liftosaur.com/blog/posts/liftosaur-overview/)

**JEFIT**
- Adaptive AI plan is Elite-only. Professional plans are in Elite. — [JEFIT vs Caliber](https://www.jefit.com/blog/jefit-vs-caliber); [JustGains](https://justgains.com/learn/jefit-alternatives)

**Ladder**
- About 14 coach-led team programs in 6-week progressive blocks. Everyone on a team does the same workout and only loads are individual. The program does not adapt to logged sets or wearable data. Home and gym programs. — [Gymadapt Ladder](https://gymadapt.com/learn/ladder-workout-app/); [Garage Gym Reviews Ladder](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/ladder-app-review); [Cora Health Ladder](https://www.corahealth.app/compare/ladder)

**Future / Caliber (human coaching)**
- Future: one certified human coach writes the program, adjusts it weekly and messages in the app. — [Pocket-Fit Future vs Caliber](https://www.pocket-fit.app/blog/future-vs-caliber); [SensAI Future review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/future-app-review-2026)
- Caliber: free app with coach-designed plans. 1-on-1 Premium includes a dedicated coach, training and nutrition plans, messaging and video form reviews, structured as a 3-month program. — [Trainwell Caliber alternatives](https://www.trainwell.net/blog/best-caliber-alternatives); [BarBend Caliber review](https://barbend.com/caliber-fitness-app-review/)

**Freeletics**
- The Coach is an adaptive AI that reacts to feedback and adjusts the next workout. It is mainly bodyweight/HIIT-focused with no live coaching. The company is GDPR-compliant and based in Munich. — [Men's Health DE](https://www.menshealth.de/sport-outdoor/fitness-abo-falle-diese-3-apps-lohnen-sich-wirklich/); [FitProTools](https://fitprotools.de/directory/freeletics/)

**Apple Fitness+**
- Custom Plans (iOS 26+) are built from the activities, durations and trainers you use, in two modes: "Stay Consistent" and "Push Further". Strength is one of 12 workout types. Germany is a supported country. — [Apple Fitness+ (IE)](https://www.apple.com/ie/apple-fitness-plus/)
- iOS 26 / watchOS 26 added a free custom workout builder and the Workout Buddy feature in the Fitness app, separate from the paid Fitness+. — [DC Rainmaker](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2025/09/apple-watch-workout-builder-ios26-watchos26.html)

**Gymshark Training**
- 450+ free workouts and programmed plans (gym and home, HIIT, lifting, stretching, mobility, meditation follow-along videos), plus the Gymshark66 habit program. — [Gymshark blog](https://uk.gymshark.com/blog/article/the-gymshark-training-app-for-ios-and-android); [Gymshark App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gymshark-training-and-fitness/id1139151320)

**Runna / Strava**
- Runna plans include strength and mobility sessions. — [Running Westward Ho](https://www.runningwestwardho.co.uk/post/runna-vs-strava); [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/10/28/runna-vs-strava/)

### Inferences
- Recovery-aware programming exists in only two flavours: muscle-fatigue estimates from logged volume (Fitbod) and subjective per-session feedback (RP: pump, soreness, joint pain). No major app was found to use HRV or sleep for strength programming. This is an open niche for an app with health-data integration.
- Equipment or gym profiles (Fitbod "Locations", Hevy gym tagging) and exercise substitution are becoming standard in the AI-planner tier.
- Program library depth is Boostcamp's moat. Scriptable logic is Liftosaur's moat. A German app could compete with curated German-language coach programs.

### Gaps
- No confirmed details on "swap exercise when equipment is busy" flows per app (Fitbod reportedly lets you replace exercises, but this was not cited in the results).
- No confirmed program import or export formats (CSV, Strong-to-Hevy import) from the sources found.
- No sources found on mesocycle or deload features in Hevy Trainer.

## 3. Analytics (muscle volume, heat maps, PRs, strength standards, body measurements, recovery)

### Takeaway
Per-muscle volume or heat maps and strength scores are the main premium analytics hooks (Boostcamp Pro, Hevy Pro sets per muscle group, Fitbod strength score and recovery heat map). PRs and estimated 1RM are free almost everywhere.

### Cited Findings
- Hevy: muscle-group activity charts (free) and set counts per muscle group (Pro). The free tier shows about 3 months of graph history; Pro gives all-time charts and all body measurements (free restricts some, for example biceps circumference). — [Hevy Help 2026 Features Guide](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/33106320824727-Everything-You-Need-to-Know-About-the-Hevy-App-2026-Features-Guide); [Push/Pull vs Hevy](https://push-pull.app/blog/push-pull-vs-hevy); [AnyGen Hevy review](https://www.anygen.io/showcase/hevy-app-review/index.html)
- Hevy gap: no overview of all workouts over a period, no recovery tracking, no HRV or sleep data, no nutrition. — [AIToolsBakery Hevy](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/hevy-review/); [Push/Pull vs Hevy](https://push-pull.app/blog/push-pull-vs-hevy)
- Fitbod: overall Strength Score and per-muscle scores (0 to 100+), estimated 1RM charts for main lifts, and a muscle recovery heat map. — [Stronger vs Fitbod](https://www.strongermobileapp.com/compare/fitbod); [SensAI Fitbod review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/fitbod-review-2026)
- Boostcamp: PRs, estimated 1RMs and a weekly "Sunday report" are free. Strength Score and a per-muscle volume heat map are Pro. — [Boostcamp Pro](https://www.boostcamp.app/pro); [Boostcamp free](https://www.boostcamp.app/free-workout-app)
- Strong PRO: advanced charts. — [SensAI Strong review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/strong-app-review-2026)
- Alpha Progression Pro: "advanced analytics", including muscle-level analytics. — [Alpha Progression Pro worth it](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/alpha-progression-pro-worth-it)
- Liftosaur premium: graphs and week insights. Body measurements are premium per one review. — [Liftosaur overview](https://www.liftosaur.com/blog/posts/liftosaur-overview/); [SaaSpartout](https://saaspartout.com/marketplace/liftosaur/)
- JEFIT Elite: advanced analytics. — [SensAI Jefit vs Hevy](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/jefit-vs-hevy-2026)

### Inferences
- A "weekly report", a per-muscle heat map and a single strength score are the visual analytics users now expect. Combining Myhealth's health data (weight, sleep) with training analytics would go beyond Hevy, which reviewers criticize for lacking recovery, sleep and nutrition context.

### Gaps
- Progress photos: no confirmed per-app source found.
- Strength standards (bodyweight-relative rankings such as the Strength Level style): no source found tying them to specific apps.

## 4. Exercise library (size, video/3D, form cues)

### Takeaway
Library sizes range from about 400 (Hevy) to about 795 (Alpha Progression), 1,000+ (Fitbod) and about 1,400 (JEFIT). Video demos are standard. Some apps gate videos behind payment (JEFIT Elite); Alpha Progression includes all videos for free.

### Cited Findings
- Hevy: 400+ exercises (third-party figure), "high quality exercise videos", 7 custom exercises on free, unlimited on Pro. — [HotelGyms Hevy guide](https://www.hotelgyms.com/blog/how-to-use-the-hevy-app); [Hevy homepage](https://www.hevyapp.com/); [Push/Pull vs Hevy](https://push-pull.app/blog/push-pull-vs-hevy)
- Alpha Progression: 795 built-in exercises with real video demonstrations and instructions, all free. — [Alpha Progression homepage](https://alphaprogression.com/en); [Alpha Progression best muscle-building apps](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/best-muscle-building-apps)
- Fitbod: 1,000+ exercises with instructions and demos. No source mentions 3D. — [Fitnessdrum Fitbod](https://fitnessdrum.com/fitbod-review/)
- JEFIT: about 1,400 exercises (cardio, HIIT, strength). The free tier has the full library, but video demonstrations are Elite. — [JEFIT best workout apps](https://www.jefit.com/blog/best-workout-apps-for-2026-top-7-options-tested-and-reviewed); [SensAI Jefit vs Hevy](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/jefit-vs-hevy-2026)
- RP Hypertrophy: 250+ technique videos. — [Mesostrength RP review](https://mesostrength.com/blog/rp-hypertrophy-app-review)
- Freeletics Training Coach: 900+ exercises (DE source). — [FitProTools](https://fitprotools.de/directory/freeletics/)
- Gymshark: video and audio demonstrations, train-along videos with athletes. — [Gymshark blog](https://uk.gymshark.com/blog/article/the-gymshark-training-app-for-ios-and-android)
- Hevy added HYROX exercises in 2026 (unofficial timeline). — [SensAI Hevy review](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/hevy-review-2026)

### Inferences
- About 400 to 800 well-filmed exercises is enough to compete (Hevy wins with about 400). Video quality and German-language form cues matter more than raw count for a German app.

### Gaps
- 3D animations: not confirmed for any app in the results. Muscle diagrams per exercise: not confirmed per app.

### Takeaway
Apple Watch logging is near table stakes (Hevy free, Strong, Fitbod, JEFIT Elite, Ladder). Wear OS is rarer (Hevy most consistently, JEFIT). Apple Health and Health Connect sync is common. Live Activities and Dynamic Island are confirmed for Alpha Progression.

### Cited Findings

### Gaps

## 6. Social, community and coach/client features

### Takeaway
Hevy is the social leader (feed, likes, comments, following, routine sharing, and a B2B "Hevy Coach" product). Ladder uses team-based social features (team chat, leaderboards, weekly challenges). Strong, Alpha Progression and Gymshark have little or no social layer.

### Cited Findings
- Hevy: a feed of followed users with likes and comments (the feed can be turned off). Sources conflict on leaderboards: some say global leaderboards exist, another says the social layer is only a feed of the people you follow. — [AIToolsBakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/hevy-review/); [Saylift Liftoff vs Hevy](https://saylift.app/alternatives/liftoff-vs-hevy); [Hevy tutorial](https://www.hevyapp.com/hevy-tutorial/)
- Hevy Coach (for trainers): coaches write programs, assign them to clients and review client workouts. Clients get Hevy Pro free. Pricing is $25/month for 1–10 clients, $50 for 11–25, $90 for 26–50 and $160 for 51–100, with a 30-day trial and no card. — [SoftwareAdvice Hevy Coach](https://www.softwareadvice.com/personal-trainer/hevy-coach-profile/); [Coachway Hevy Coach review](https://coachway.io/articles/hevy-coach-review/); [Hevy Coach workout builder](https://hevycoach.com/features/workout-builder/)
- Ladder: teams, team chat, badges, team leaderboards and weekly challenges. Monthly PRO gives 1 team (2 team changes per month); annual unlocks all teams. — [Gymadapt](https://gymadapt.com/learn/ladder-workout-app/); [Cora Health](https://www.corahealth.app/compare/ladder)
- JEFIT: community access in the free tier. — [SensAI Jefit vs Hevy](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/jefit-vs-hevy-2026)
- Boostcamp: user-published programs (about 13,900 of its 14,000+). — [Boostcamp program library](https://www.boostcamp.app/features/program-library)
- Gymshark: no leaderboards, forums or social elements, but in-person class booking in select locations. — [Softwr Gymshark](https://www.softwr.com/software/gymshark-training); [TikTok review](https://www.tiktok.com/@sheaaa.butterr/video/7310240392593607941)
- Future and Caliber: in-app coach messaging. Caliber adds video form reviews. — [Trainwell Caliber alternatives](https://www.trainwell.net/blog/best-caliber-alternatives)

### Inferences
- A coach/client mode, like Hevy Coach, is a B2B revenue channel no German-focused tracker seems to own. Note that this is inferred, not verified.

### Gaps
- Challenges in Hevy, Strava clubs and strength challenges: not researched in depth.

## 7. Gamification (streaks, badges, levels, share cards)

### Takeaway
Mainstream lifting trackers keep gamification light: Hevy has a streak counter, Fitbod has streaks and milestone badges, JEFIT has points and badges, and Ladder has badges. Deep gamification (XP, levels, trophies) exists only in niche apps such as IronStreak.

### Cited Findings
- Hevy: streak counter, shallow gamification, "no unnecessary gamification". — [Ironstreak gamified apps](https://ironstreak.com/blog/best-gamified-workout-apps); [Stronger best trackers](https://www.strongermobileapp.com/blog/best-workout-tracker-apps)

- Ladder: badges. Gymshark: Gymshark66 habit challenge. — [Gymadapt](https://gymadapt.com/learn/ladder-workout-app/); [Gymshark](https://uk.gymshark.com/blog/article/the-gymshark-training-app-for-ios-and-android)

### Inferences
- Shareable workout summary cards and PR celebrations are expected (Hevy's social feed relies on them), but heavy gamification is not a buying reason for serious lifters.

### Gaps
- Share-card designs and per-app PR-celebration behavior were not confirmed in sources.

## 9. User sentiment (praise, complaints, requests)

### Takeaway
Users praise fast, clean logging (Strong, Hevy), price and social features (Hevy), and the free program library (Boostcamp). They complain about free-tier caps (Hevy's routine limit), Fitbod's price and weight suggestions, RP's very high price, and the lack of recovery, sleep and nutrition context in trackers. Direct Reddit data could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Hevy has a 4.9-star average from about 94k ratings on the US App Store. — [Push/Pull vs Hevy](https://push-pull.app/blog/push-pull-vs-hevy)
- Hevy complaints: free routine cap; black-screen bugs after an update (older review, about 18 months old); the rest-timer and reorder bugs noted in section 1; missing period overviews; no recovery, HRV, sleep or nutrition data. — [Dr-Muscle Hevy review](https://dr-muscle.com/hevy-workout-app-review/); [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hevy-workout-tracker-gym-log/id1458862350?see-all=reviews&platform=watch); [AIToolsBakery](https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/hevy-review/)
- Strong is praised as "the cleanest workout logger". Hevy is described as "basically Strong with a social layer". — [Getfitcraft DE](https://getfitcraft.com/de/compare/best-strength-training-apps)
- Fitbod (Reddit roundup, about 1.5 years old): unsure about weight and rep suggestions (users need to raise weights), trouble seeing results, high cost, poor support. — [Dr-Muscle Fitbod Reddit roundup](https://dr-muscle.com/fitbod-review-reddit/)
- Ladder: users dislike that everyone on a team does the same workout with no adaptation. — [Gymadapt](https://gymadapt.com/learn/ladder-workout-app/)
- Boostcamp: reviewers consider the free tier enough for most lifters. — [Fortune Boostcamp review](https://fortune.com/article/boostcamp-review/)
- No independent German test (Stiftung Warentest, Computerbild) of strength apps for 2026 was found. German rankings are mostly vendor-written (Alpha Progression ranks itself #1). — [iosapps.de](https://iosapps.de/apps/vergleich/fitness-apps/); [Alpha Progression DE ranking](https://alphaprogression.com/en/blog/best-strength-training-apps)

### Inferences
- The recurring unmet need across sources is a tracker that is as fast as Hevy or Strong but also uses recovery, sleep and nutrition context for programming. An integrated health app like Myhealth could position itself exactly there.

### Gaps
- Direct Reddit threads (r/Hevy, r/fitbod, r/weightroom) could not be searched or fetched; the search tool returned no Reddit results. Most-requested-feature lists are therefore unverified.
- Recent 2026 App Store and Play Store review analyses for Alpha Progression, JEFIT and Boostcamp were not found.
